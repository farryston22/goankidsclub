Goan Kids Club

Privacy notice

Last updated 18 November 2026

In short: children can play every game without signing up. There are no adverts, no tracking and no analytics. Progress is kept on your own device. It only goes online if a grown-up chooses to turn on Save progress online.

What we keep on your device

To remember progress, the games save these details in your browser's storage on your device:

  • each child's first name (or nickname), the buddy picture they chose and their age group
  • stars, stickers, stages reached, the Goan house they designed and roughly how long they have played
  • sound and voice settings

This stays on your device. Clearing your browser's data for this site deletes it.

If you turn on "Save progress online"

This is optional and only a grown-up can switch it on, in the Grown-ups area. If you do:

  • The details listed above are copied to our online database so you can keep them safe and use them on other devices.
  • You get a secret family code. There is no email address, password or account. We do not know who you are.
  • The code is stored in scrambled (hashed) form. Anyone who has your code can load your children's first names and progress, so please keep it private, just like a password.
  • The data is stored with our database provider Supabase, in London (UK).
  • We use it only to give your progress back to you. We never sell or share it, and never use it for adverts.

We use this information because you ask us to (your consent). You can change your mind at any time.

Deleting your data

  • Online copy: Grown-ups area → Save progress online → Delete online copy. It is deleted straight away.
  • One child: Grown-ups area → Children → Delete.
  • Everything on a device: clear this website's data in your browser settings.

Other things to know

  • The page loads its letter shapes (fonts) from Google Fonts, which can see your device's internet address, as it does for most websites.
  • The talking voice uses the voice built into your device. Nothing your child says or does is recorded.
  • The games are designed for children aged 3 to 6, played with a grown-up nearby.
  • The "Support" and "WhatsApp us" buttons are for grown-ups. They open PayPal or WhatsApp, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to them unless you tap a button.
